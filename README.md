<h1>OmniPanel</h1>
<p>OmniPanel is an app for desktops to simulate inputs, mainly targetting secondary touchscreen monitors to be used while playing games.</p>
<p>The goal is to make controlling your game easy, no more remembering all kinds of keybinds, just a couple of grapical buttons on your screen.</p>
<h3>How?</h3>
<p>By making OmniPanel not steal focus. This way you can keep playing your game like normal and use your touchscreen like it was a seperate input device</p>
<h5>Disclaimer, OmniPanel is still under development and will recieve frequent updates that might break your setup.</h5>
